## MingCute Icons

No paid Pro icons or bundled font assets are included.

## NEAR symbol
The vector shape in `near-symbol.svg` is based on the NEAR mark published in
the open-source Simple Icons project:
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/near.svg

NEAR and its logo are associated with NEAR Protocol and remain subject to
applicable brand/trademark usage rules. The app's use of the NEAR logo is an
ecosystem/platform reference, not an assertion that the example manufacturer or
RecallBond product is officially endorsed by NEAR Protocol.

## Isocons decorative icon
The decorative SVG `isocons-wallet.svg` is adapted from the **Wallet** isometric
icon by **Oyeleye Ogunsanya and Moses Enyinnaya** on https://www.isocons.app/.
The SVG line colour and stroke weight were adjusted to match RecallBond.

Licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)**:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Credit is also provided in the public landing page footer. The icon is used
sparingly as supporting artwork, not as a replacement for the interface icons.

## Typography
The refined product UI uses **Inter** from Google Fonts; prior visual
explorations used DM Sans, Space Grotesk and Manrope. This selection is informed
by the restrained typography of wallet UIs but does not copy proprietary fonts.
No font files are bundled.
