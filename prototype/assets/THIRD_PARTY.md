## NEAR symbol

the open-source Simple Icons project:
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/near.svg

NEAR and its logo are associated with NEAR Protocol and remain subject to
applicable brand/trademark usage rules. The app's use of the NEAR logo is an
ecosystem/platform reference, not an assertion that the example manufacturer or
RecallBond product is officially endorsed by NEAR Protocol.

## Typography
The UI uses **Manrope** from Google Fonts (displayed via CSS), as a design
exploration inspired by clean wallet interfaces. The exact font used by Solflare's
expanded browser extension has **not** been independently verified or copied.
No font files are bundled in this repository.
