03 / ENGINEERING NOTES

A real contract.
A clear boundary.

Onchain ownership and financial settlement are real testnet actions. The public consumer and manufacturer screens are illustrative sample experiences, not proof of account-signed browser transactions.

RecallBond also provides experimental account-linked signing and a restricted administrator console. Browser-side sponsorship and complete account-signed E2E testing remain an explicit release gate.
